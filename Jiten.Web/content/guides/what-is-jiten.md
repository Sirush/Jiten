Jiten analyses Japanese media, including visual novels, books, anime, dramas, and games, and turns each one into detailed vocabulary statistics and a ready-to-study Anki deck. If you learn Japanese through immersion, Jiten helps you answer two questions: **"what should I read or watch next?"** and **"which words do I need to learn for it?"**

This guide walks through the core concepts. Once they click, the rest of the site is easy to navigate.

## Media decks

A **deck** is one piece of media that has been parsed into its vocabulary. Every deck page shows the words that appear in it, how often each one occurs, and a frequency-ordered list you can study from. Decks can have parent and child relationships, like a series and its individual volumes or episodes.

::tip
Browse everything from the **Media** page in the header. Filter by type, difficulty, or length to find something at your level.
::

## Known words & coverage

As you learn, you mark words as **known**. Jiten tracks these per account, so every deck can be measured against what _you_ already know.

**Coverage** is the headline number: the percentage of the words in a deck that you already know. A higher coverage means an easier, more comfortable read.

- **Around 80%** coverage is challenging, and you will be looking up a lot.
- **Around 90%** coverage is a good immersion sweet spot for most learners.
- **98% and above** is comfortable, mostly for review and enjoyment.

::note
You do not have to mark words one by one. You can import a list of known words (for example, exported from another tool), and coverage across the whole site updates automatically.
::

## Difficulty

Every deck gets a **difficulty score** from a model trained on Japanese text. It lets you compare media on a single scale instead of guessing from the summary. Combined with coverage, character count, and word count, it gives you a realistic picture of how hard something will be _for you_ before you commit.

## Studying with the SRS

Once you have found media at your level, you can study its vocabulary with Jiten's built-in **SRS** (spaced-repetition system), powered by FSRS. Reviews are scheduled so you see each word right before you would forget it. You can also generate an **Anki deck** and study in Anki instead, whichever fits your workflow.
