You can customise a lot of what Jiten looks like on the **Display** settings page: furigana, fonts, text sizes, word colours, stats on the media card, and more. With an account, you can save your choices in a **profile**, so you can carry them from one device to another, or switch them on the fly.

## Where to find it

You can access a quick panel by hovering the gear button at the top-right of the header on desktop, or the **Display** button at the bottom of the hamburger menu on mobile. It holds some settings that you might want to change often and a link to the full settings page.

You can access the full set of options by clicking **More display settings** at the bottom of that panel or, while connected, going to your **[settings page](/settings)** and **[display](/settings/display)**.

Every change applies straight away, on both. On the Display page, **Find a setting** at the top narrows the page down to the settings that match what you type.

## Profiles

A profile is a way to save your settings and reuse them on other devices, or switch them.

Your profile is set per device, so you can for example have a different font size on desktop and on mobile. Your profiles are saved to your account.

- **New** creates a profile, either as **A copy of the current settings** or from **Jiten’s default settings**, and switches this device to it.
- **Rename** and **Delete** the current profile. You can't undo a deletion.
- **Export** saves the profile to a file that you can then **Import** back or share with someone else.

If you're not logged in, settings are saved in this browser only and offer a limited set of options.

## Restore defaults

Each section has its own **Reset** button next to its title to reset the section settings to the defaults.

There's also a global **Restore defaults** at the top of the page that will reset your entire profile back to Jiten's default settings.

What an SRS card shows, with its own furigana and text size options, is set separately in **Settings → Study (SRS)**. See [Using the SRS](/guides/using-the-srs).
